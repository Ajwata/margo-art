About the Artist

I live in Ukraine and hold a degree as a certified composer. Painting has always been my true passion and the calling of my life. I have been working professionally in fine art for more than 20 years, and for the past decade I have been dedicated to abstract painting.

My works are distinguished by vibrant, dynamic energy and bold color combinations, often enhanced with gold leaf. Collectors and clients often share that my art, with its joyful vitality, helps them overcome feelings of melancholy and even lifts them out of depression.

I believe that art should bring beauty and strength, inspire a zest for life, and provide deep visual pleasure. Through my paintings, I strive to share this energy with people all over the world.

FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

Do you ship worldwide?
Yes. Paintings are carefully packed in multiple protective layers (kraft, film, box/tube), tracking is provided. Delivery time depends on the destination country.
Can I commission a painting?
Yes. You can choose the size, palette, and theme. Message me on Instagram or Telegram — we’ll discuss references, deadlines, and cost.
Contacts: Instagram @margo_v_art · Telegram @margaritavityaz · Email margarita​vityaz@gmail.com
What payment methods do you accept?
Bank transfer and online payment options are available (depending on your country). A deposit is required to reserve a commission.
Do the works come with a certificate of authenticity?
Each original painting is accompanied by a signed certificate with the title and year of creation.
Do you provide framing?
By default, paintings come on stretched canvas without a frame. Framing can be arranged upon request for an additional fee.
Can I return or exchange a painting?
Original artworks are generally non-returnable, but I’m always open to dialogue. If damage occurs during shipping, I will assist in resolving it with the carrier/insurance.
How should I care for my painting?
Avoid direct sunlight and excessive humidity. Dust gently with a soft dry cloth. Paintings are finished with a protective varnish.
